Decision Brief

Build vs. buy: billing system

5-seat council · July 19, 2026 · Consensus reached (avg 8.75/10)

Recommendation

Adopt Stripe Billing before the January sunset, and build a small internal usage-metering table alongside it. Do not build a full in-house billing system.

Rationale

  • 3-year TCO strongly favors buying: ~$95k in fees vs ~$450k to build and maintain (Sage, unchallenged after Round 1).
  • January deadline makes a build schedule-infeasible with 2 backend engineers (Atlas; supported by Scout's overrun data).
  • The one real risk of buying — lock-in on usage-based pricing — is neutralized by owning the metering data internally (Critic's amendment, adopted unanimously).

Key contention & resolution

Critic initially argued for a thin in-house build to preserve pricing flexibility. After conceding the TCO math, Critic narrowed the claim to metering ownership, which the council adopted into the final proposal.

Dissents & reservations

Critic (8/10): "If Stripe's usage-meter pricing changes materially, revisit this decision at renewal."

Next steps

  • Spike Stripe Billing migration this sprint (Forge suggested owner).
  • Design the usage-metering table schema — est. 1 week.
  • Set a renewal-review reminder for the Stripe contract.
